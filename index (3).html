What is Have You Seen This?

This is a personal blog built around one habit — reading Scripture slowly enough to notice the things that don't quite make sense at first glance, and then sitting with the question instead of rushing past it.

No theology degree. No pulpit. Just an open Bible and genuine curiosity.

New questions, every week

No devotionals. No fluff. Just the verses people skip past — and the questions hiding inside them.
